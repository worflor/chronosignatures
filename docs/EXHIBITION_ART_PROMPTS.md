# The Chronosignatures Pavilion: Continuous Architectural Exhibition Master Art Prompts

This document serves as the master curatorial blueprint, architectural continuity guide, and prompt archive for the complete visual suite of **CHRONOSIGNATURES** (Tracks 01–06 plus the Gesaffelstein Cyber-Industrial Ghost Remix and LE SSERAFIM Style Remix).

It is specifically engineered for generation on state-of-the-art image models (e.g. **FLUX.1 [dev/schnell]**, **Midjourney v6**, **Imagen 3**, **DALL-E 3**) to achieve two critical goals:
1. **True Physical Continuity ("The Exhibition Walkthrough")**: Every artwork is situated inside a single, contiguous modernist museum pavilion designed in the tradition of Tadao Ando, Peter Zumthor, and John Pawson. Hallways, reflecting pools, materials, and vistas physically connect from one image to the next, featuring visible background landmarks of adjacent rooms.
2. **Text-Free Pristine Quality**: All typography commands have been removed to eliminate garbled pseudo-lettering and AI text artifacts, producing pure gallery-grade architectural photography.

### Shared Architectural DNA & Continuity Elements:
- **Walls**: Smooth board-formed basalt concrete with subtle horizontal wood-grain formwork texture and recessed tie-rod holes.
- **Flooring**: Continuous dark honed charcoal terrazzo / polished slate with subtle aggregate specks, reflecting soft directional light.
- **Water Feature**: A continuous 40cm-deep indoor reflecting pool with black slate bottom that originates in Gallery 1 and flows through Galleries 2, 3, and 4.
- **Perimeter Lighting**: Continuous linear LED reveal channels recessed into the baseboards along the floor edges, casting a soft 5cm ambient wash.
- **Plinths**: Monolithic, cantilevered blocks of matte black basalt stone or raw cleft granite.
- **Camera Style**: Hasselblad H6D-100c, 35mm f/4 tilt-shift architectural lens, eye-level perspective, crisp focus, raytraced glass refractions and natural caustic patterns.
